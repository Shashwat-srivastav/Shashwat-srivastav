# 💫 About Me

I work on **algorithmic trading and quantitative research**, with a focus on **how decisions propagate through markets**.

## 🔭 Current Focus
- Algorithmic trading strategies grounded in **market microstructure, regime shifts, and adaptive position sizing** (Kelly-inspired, not naïve Kelly)
- Research-grade experiments at the intersection of **quant finance, ML, and decision-making under uncertainty**
- End-to-end systems: **data → signal → backtest → risk → execution** (no toy notebooks, no curve-fitting theater)

## 👯 Collaboration Interests
- **Quant research**: alpha signals, execution models, market impact, volatility & regime modeling  
- **ML-for-finance papers** with publishable ambition (NeurIPS / ICML / AISTATS level)  
- Serious **open-source** work in numerical methods, optimization, or trading infrastructure  

> If it can’t be backtested, falsified, or measured, it’s not research.

## 🤝 Looking For
- Strong feedback on **research rigor** (assumptions → experiments → failure analysis)  
- Review from people who’ve shipped **real-money strategies or publishable work**  
- Brutal critiques of math, code, and reasoning — politeness adds no value here

## 🌱 Currently Learning
- **Probability, stochastic processes, and optimization** for quant roles  
- **Deep learning & reinforcement learning** for sequential decision problems  
- How elite researchers structure problems *before* touching code

## 💬 Ask Me About
- Quant trading competitions & live strategy design  
- Turning **non-finance intuition into tradable signals**  
- Building ML systems fast **without sacrificing correctness**
